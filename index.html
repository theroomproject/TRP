All prices shown on this website are rough planning estimates only and are not confirmed quotations. Meet the Room Raiders

Every project needs someone in charge.

Room Raiders are TRP-employed project supervisors assigned to customer transformations. They are not a replacement for licensed specialist trades. Their job is to make sure the right people, materials and decisions come together properly.

  • Coordinate contractors, suppliers and scheduled site access
  • Track project progress against the agreed scope
  • Act as the customer’s primary project contact
  • Flag issues, changes and approvals before they become surprises
  • Conduct practical quality and finishing checks
  • Coordinate final styling and handover
Launch team: TRP plans to begin with approximately 2–5 Room Raiders and scale the supervision team as project volume grows.

Questions before we raid the room
TRP FAQ

        

          
Do I need an empty room before TRP can help?
No. TRP can assess furnished, outdated, cluttered, unfinished or mostly empty rooms. What stays, moves, gets disposed of or gets replaced is agreed during scoping.

          
Are the website prices fixed?
No. They are rough from-scratch planning estimates only. A final quotation is issued after the real room and scope are assessed and TRP’s relevant specialist partners confirm their costs and availability.

          
Who actually performs renovation and specialist works?
TRP coordinates appropriate contractors, suppliers and specialist service providers for the agreed project. Works requiring appropriately qualified or licensed trades are assigned accordingly. Your Room Raider supervises coordination and progress rather than pretending to be every trade.

          
Can I mix several room concepts?
Yes. A customer can combine styles — for example, a dark-luxury gaming bedroom, a Bali-inspired meditation room, or a content-creator studio with sneaker displays. The categories are starting points, not restrictions.

          
Can TRP work around my budget?
That is the intention. TRP can prioritise the highest-impact items and recommend a practical scope. Final feasibility still depends on the condition of the room and actual partner/supplier pricing.

          
Will my project have one person I can contact?
TRP’s operating model is to assign a Room Raider as the project supervisor and primary project contact, supported by the wider TRP team and specialist partners.
